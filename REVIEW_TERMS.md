# Private review terms

This repository is prepared for private mathematical review. It does not grant
a public redistribution or publication license for the manuscript or project
notes. Authorship and a durable citation form have not yet been finalized.

Third-party papers and the external Lean development are not redistributed.
They remain governed by their original licenses and are referenced only by
citations, pinned revisions, and checksums.

Reviewers may quote short passages as needed for private mathematical feedback.
Before sharing the complete package, publishing a derivative, or citing the
result as established, contact the repository owner and verify the current
status.
